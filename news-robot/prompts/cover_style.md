Для модели изображения (по-английски, всегда в промпте):
- black ink drawing, pen and brush, in the manner of Yuri Annenkov's 1918 illustrations for Alexander Blok's poem "The Twelve";
- stark black ink on off-white paper, no grey wash, plus ONE flat accent of our brick red #A9381F (the red of the Vh² ribbon) — a single bold shape or band: a flag, a stripe, a plane of a building facet, a screen; no other colors;
- angular cubo-futurist fragmentation, sharp diagonals, broken planes, dynamic tilted composition;
- dense hatching and cross-hatching for shadows, bold solid black masses against white paper;
- figures as expressive silhouettes, city and architecture as shards and facets;
- slightly aged off-white paper texture, hand-drawn line, visible brush strokes;
- no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealism, no 3D render look, no gradients.
